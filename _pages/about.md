I have an undergraduate background in machine learning, deep learning, and reinforcement learning. I work primarily with **Python and PyTorch**, with project experience spanning image generation, language model adaptation, and image classification.

My undergraduate thesis focused on **continuous image diffusion**. I have also worked with **ChatGLM2-6B using LoRA, Prefix tuning, and p-tuning**, built a custom VGG model for CIFAR-10, and implemented parallel KNN.

Alongside modeling, I have experience with Linux, Hadoop, Spark, SQL, and MySQL. My interests connect learning algorithms with the systems used to run them.
